# Trademarking SWIP — step by step

> Written for India, because that is where you are. Every step has the actual
> link to click. Nothing here needs a lawyer, though §7 says when one is worth it.
>
> **This is not legal advice.** It is the process, accurately described.

---

## 1. What you are actually protecting

Three different things, and they are **separate filings**:

| # | What | Filed as | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The name `SWIP`** as plain text | **Word mark** | 🔴 **Highest.** Protects the name in any font, any colour |
| 2 | **The logo** — the slanted gold foil wordmark | **Device mark** | 🟡 Medium — protects that specific artwork |
| 3 | The idea — MCC capture over NFC | **Not trademarkable** | See §8 |

**File the word mark first.** A device mark only protects the artwork; someone
could launch "SWIP" in a different typeface and you would have little recourse.
A word mark covers the name however it is written, which is what actually
matters.

---

## 2. Which classes

Trademarks are registered per **class** of goods/services, and you pay per class.

| Class | Covers | Do you need it? |
|---|---|---|
| **Class 9** | Downloadable software, mobile apps | ✅ **Yes — essential.** SWIP is a downloadable app ([ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/mobile-app-trademark-registration-class)) |
| **Class 42** | SaaS, software development, cloud services | ✅ **Yes.** Covers the service side and any future backend ([LegalWiz](https://www.legalwiz.in/blog/trademark-class-42-technology-research-and-software-services)) |
| **Class 36** | Financial and banking services | ⚠️ **Yes, if v2 happens.** The moment SWIP holds money, issues a card, or sells travel credit, this is the class that protects it |

**Recommendation: file Classes 9 and 42 now.** Add 36 when the money features
become real — filing a class you do not yet operate in risks the registration
being challenged for non-use after five years.

Cost is **per class**, so 9 + 42 is two filings' worth of fees.

---

## 3. Search first — free, do it today

**Before you spend anything**, check nobody already holds "SWIP" in your classes.

**→ [Public Trade Mark Search](https://tmrsearch.ipindia.gov.in/tmrpublicsearch/)**

How to use it:

1. Choose **Wordmark** search.
2. Search type: **Start With**, then repeat with **Contains**.
3. Enter `SWIP`. Search Class **9**, then repeat for **42** and **36**.
4. Repeat the whole thing for the near-misses that would still block you:
   **SWIPE**, **SWIP E**, **SWIIP**, **ZWIP**, **SWYP**.

> ⚠️ **"Swipe" is dense in fintech.** That is the real risk with this name — not
> an exact "SWIP" collision, but a registered **SWIPE** mark in Class 9 or 36
> whose owner argues SWIP is deceptively similar. The Registry applies a
> "likelihood of confusion" test, not an exact-match test.
>
> Search **SWIPE** in classes 9, 36 and 42 before anything else. If a large
> fintech holds it, get 30 minutes of a trademark attorney's time before filing.

Also worth checking, since you will want these internationally later:

- **[WIPO Global Brand Database](https://branddb.wipo.int/)** — worldwide
- **[USPTO TESS](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/)** — United States
- **[EUIPO eSearch](https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/)** — Europe

## 4. What you need before filing

| Item | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Class 3 Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)** | Any [licensed CA](https://cca.gov.in/licensed_ca.html) — eMudhra, Sify, VSign | ~₹1,500–3,000, 1–2 days. **Required** for e-filing |
| **IP India e-filing account** | [ipindiaonline.gov.in](https://ipindiaonline.gov.in/trademarkefiling/user/frmLoginNew.aspx) | Free |
| **The mark** | For a word mark: just the text `SWIP` | For a device mark: the logo as JPG, max 8cm × 8cm |
| **Applicant details** | Your name + address, or the company's | See §5 — this choice matters |
| **DPIIT / Udyam certificate** | [Startup India](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/) or [Udyam](https://udyamregistration.gov.in/) | **Halves your fee.** See §5 |

## 5. The one decision that halves your cost

Government fee per class, per [IP India](https://ipindia.gov.in/fee-structure-tm.htm):

| Applicant type | Fee per class |
|---|---|
| **Individual, DPIIT-recognised startup, or Udyam-registered MSME** | **₹4,500** |
| Company / LLP / anyone else | **₹9,000** |

So for Classes 9 + 42:

- As an individual or registered startup: **₹9,000 total**
- As an ordinary company: **₹18,000 total**

> **Get Udyam registration first — it is free and takes about ten minutes**
> at [udyamregistration.gov.in](https://udyamregistration.gov.in/). It halves
> your trademark fee immediately and costs nothing. If you are also going the
> company route for the Play Store (see
> [13-PLAY-STORE-LAUNCH §2](13-PLAY-STORE-LAUNCH.md#2-decide-this-before-you-pay-personal-or-organisation)),
> do Udyam at the same time.
>
> **File in the name that will own the app long-term.** Moving a trademark from
> you personally to your company later is a separate assignment filing with its
> own fee and paperwork.

## 6. Filing — the actual steps

1. **Log in** to [IP India e-filing](https://ipindiaonline.gov.in/trademarkefiling/user/frmLoginNew.aspx) with your DSC.
2. Choose **New Application → Form TM-A**.
3. **Applicant type** — tick Individual / Startup / MSME if it applies, and
   **upload the certificate in the same session**. You cannot claim the reduced
   fee retroactively.
4. **Mark type** → *Word Mark*. Enter `SWIP` in caps.
5. **Class** → 9. Then write the goods description. Use standard wording:
   > *"Downloadable mobile application software for identifying and recording
   > merchant category codes of payment transactions; downloadable software for
   > personal finance record-keeping."*
6. **Pay** — ₹4,500 or ₹9,000. You get an **application number instantly**.
7. **Repeat for Class 42** as a separate application (or use a multi-class
   application; the fee is the same either way).
8. **Start using ™ immediately.** You may put ™ next to SWIP from the moment you
   file. **®  is illegal until the mark is actually registered.**

## 7. What happens next, and when

| Stage | Timing | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Application number | Instant | You have a priority date. This is what matters most |
| **Examination report** | 4–8 months | The Registry may object. Very common — not fatal |
| Reply to objection | 30 days from report | **This is where an attorney earns their fee** |
| Hearing (if needed) | Varies | |
| **Journal publication** | After acceptance | Published in the Trade Marks Journal |
| **Opposition window** | **4 months** | Anyone can oppose. This is where a "SWIPE" owner would strike |
| **Registration** | 12–24 months total | Certificate issued. **Now you may use ®** |

Sources: [IP India](https://ipindia.gov.in/), [Intepat on TM-A](https://www.intepat.com/blog/file-trademark-application-india-form-tm-a).

> **Do not wait for registration to launch.** Your rights run from the
> **filing date**, not the registration date. File, then ship.

**When to hire an attorney:** if the examination report objects under §11
(similar existing mark), or if anyone opposes. Expect ₹10,000–25,000 for a
reply. Filing itself you can genuinely do yourself.

## 8. What a trademark does *not* protect

Worth being clear, because this is where founders lose money:

| You want to protect | Trademark? | What actually applies |
|---|---|---|
| The name SWIP | ✅ Yes | This document |
| The logo | ✅ Yes | Device mark, §1 |
| **The idea of reading MCC over NFC** | ❌ **No** | Would need a **patent** — and it is almost certainly unpatentable, because it uses a documented EMV mechanism exactly as specified. See [03-RESEARCH §3](03-RESEARCH-MCC-CAPTURE.md) |
| The app's source code | ❌ No | **Copyright** — automatic, no filing needed |
| The merchant graph you build | ❌ No | **Database rights + being first.** This is your real moat |

**Your defensibility is not legal, it is accumulated data.** Every capture makes
the merchant graph better, and a competitor starting in year two starts with an
empty one. That is worth more than any filing.

## 9. Also do these, today, for ₹2,000

Trademark protection is slow. Name-squatting is fast.

- **Register the domains** — `swip.app`, `swip.in`, and `swiip.com` defensively.
- **Grab the handles** — `@swipapp` on Instagram, X, LinkedIn.
- **Reserve the Play Store package name** by creating a draft listing. Package
  names are permanent and first-come — see
  [13-PLAY-STORE-LAUNCH §4.1](13-PLAY-STORE-LAUNCH.md#41-lock-the-package-name--permanent).
